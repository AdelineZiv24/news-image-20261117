1 Ukraine · War — the day's lead

Ukraine blows the Crimean Bridge apart again; Russia answers on Kyiv’s river crossings

Kyiv — Ukraine struck the Kerch Bridge linking Russia to occupied Crimea for the second time in under 24 hours, collapsing part of the roadway and closing the crossing, in what President Volodymyr Zelenskyy hailed as an “unprecedented operation”. Crimea’s Moscow-installed chief said traffic was suspended.

Russia answered late on Monday with ballistic missiles on Kyiv’s North and South bridges over the Dnipro, disrupting heating, water and power across the capital. Moscow claimed 1,441 Ukrainian drones intercepted in a single night — a figure that could not be verified.
