1 Europe · Ukraine war — the lead

Ukraine knocks a span off the Crimean Bridge for a second day

Zelenskyy hails an “unprecedented operation”; trains are running again and Moscow vows to rebuild. Russia answers on Kyiv’s crossings (16).

2 Middle East · The Gulf

Iran aims drones at a US radar in Bahrain; US jets strike back

Bahrain and the US say every drone was downed; the jets then hit Qeshm. Tehran’s mine claim is unverified.

3 Africa · Health

Ebola in DR Congo passes 11,000 cases, the WHO says

Doubled in three months; about 5,400 dead — and the Bundibugyo strain has no licensed vaccine.

4 US · Business

Nvidia’s sales double to $115.8bn

Posted after the close; $130bn guided next. The Dow fell 849 first.

5 Asia · Diplomacy

Quad leaders meet in Manila, first time since 2024

Trump, Modi, Takaichi and Albanese agree critical-minerals deals.

6 Americas · Health

US, Mexico and Bolivia lose measles-free status

PAHO ends America’s 26-year run; 5,700+ US cases this year.

7 Global · Climate

COP31’s first draft splits the Antalya talks

The fossil-fuel phase-out is the fight; talks end Friday.

8 Asia · Politics

Mongolia’s president says he will resign

Khurelsukh denies a $25m railway-bribe claim; parliament must accept.

9 Europe · Politics

Serbia’s student MPs take their seats

The Student List holds 140 of 250; Vučić’s party goes into opposition.

10 Asia · Labour

Police tear-gas Bangladesh garment workers

They want Tk 30,000 a month, up from 12,500; 37 factories shut.

11 Russia · Air crash

An-12 crash in Siberia kills all 19 aboard

Came down near Kemerovo; a technical fault, says the ministry.

12 Africa · Justice

Mozambique puts its opposition leader on trial

Five charges over the deadly 2024 protests; he denies them.

13 US · Banking

Chase software bug stalled US payments

Monday’s glitch delayed Zelle transfers across banks; fixed by evening.

14 Space · NASA

Voyager 1 closes on one light-day from Earth

It crosses early Wednesday; on Tuesday NASA asked the world to flash it a salute.

15 Middle East · Gaza

UN told Gaza’s truce risks collapse

Agencies say 1.4 million face acute hunger as winter aid is held up.
